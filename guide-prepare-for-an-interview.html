Prepare for an interview

Work, Jobs & Career

  1. Read the job advert and note what they ask for.
  2. Prepare short examples of times you did those things.
  3. Practise answering ‘Tell me about yourself’ aloud.
  4. Prepare two questions to ask them.
  5. Plan your route and arrive early.
Honest, clear answers matter more than perfect ones.

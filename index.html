LifeHelper: everyday help, step by step

LifeHelper is a free web app for everyday problem solving. Describe your situation and get a clear, practical plan you can follow today.

What you can do with LifeHelper

  • Everyday help and practical life advice for work, money, communication and wellbeing
  • Daily planning tools and productivity tools for tasks, goals and habits
  • A personal budget planner and expense tracker
  • A study helper with learning support: summaries and practice tests from your notes
  • CV builder and career preparation, including interview practice
  • A job search organizer to track applications and deadlines

LifeHelper gives general guidance only. It is not professional advice and no result is guaranteed.

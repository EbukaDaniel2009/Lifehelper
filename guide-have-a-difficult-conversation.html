Have a difficult conversation

Communication

  1. Decide what outcome you want.
  2. Choose a calm, private moment.
  3. Describe the situation and how you feel, without blaming.
  4. Listen without interrupting.
  5. Agree on one clear next step.
Be respectful and open to cultural differences.

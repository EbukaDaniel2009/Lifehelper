Prepare a simple CV

Work, Jobs & Career

  1. List your contact details, then 2–3 lines about who you are and what you offer.
  2. Write your work or volunteer experience, newest first, using action words.
  3. Add education, training and skills that match the job.
  4. Ask someone you trust to check spelling.
  5. Save it as PDF and name it clearly.

Handle everyday stress

Wellbeing & Habits

  1. Notice the stress and name it.
  2. Breathe in for 4, out for 6, five times.
  3. Write down what is in your control.
  4. Take one small action.
  5. Talk to someone you trust.
If you feel unsafe or overwhelmed, contact a doctor, counsellor or local emergency services.

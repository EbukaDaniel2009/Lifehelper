Study for an exam

Learning Practical Skills

  1. List topics and mark the ones you find hardest.
  2. Make a timetable until the exam date.
  3. Study in 25-minute blocks with short breaks.
  4. Test yourself with past questions.
  5. Sleep well before the exam.
